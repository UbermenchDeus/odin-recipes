Panera Mac and Cheese

Description

Panera Bread’s Mac & Cheese features tender medium shell pasta tossed in a ultra-rich, creamy sauce made from a blend of cheeses highlighted by a tangy, aged white cheddar cheese base

Ingredients

  • 1 pound dried medium shell pasta
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground mustard
  • 1/4 teaspoon paprika
  • 2 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 2 cups shredded extra-sharp white Cheddar cheese
  • 1 cup shredded Gruyere cheese
