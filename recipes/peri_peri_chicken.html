Peri Peri Chicken

Description

Peri peri chicken is a famous spicy dish made with African bird's eye chilies, garlic, and lemon. It originated in Mozambique and Angola through African and Portuguese roots, and is now loved all over the world.

Peri peri chicken is a popular grilled dish featuring butterflied poultry coated in a vibrant, spicy, and acidic marinade. Centered around the fiery African bird's-eye chili, the recipe blends Portuguese and Southern African culinary traditions to deliver a smoky, zesty, and juicy flavor profile

Ingredients

  • 6 gloves garlic
  • 4 red Fresno chili peppers, seeded
  • 1 habanero pepper, seeded
  • 1/2 cup diced red bell pepper, or other sweet pepper
  • 1 tablespoon smoked paprika
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon ground cayenne pepper
  • 1 teaspoon white sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 cup mild olive oil, or vegetable oil
  • 1/3 cup sherry vinegar
  • 2 lemons, juiced
  • 4 chicken leg/thigh quarters
  • kosher salt to taste to season chicken
